outbound Overview

KVDA in partnership with organizations from Africa, Asia, Europe and the Americas affords volunteering opportunity to people of all ages who are motivated to render service to needy causes with passion and selflessness. KVDA is an indigenous, non-political and membership organization which is non-sectarian and non-profit started in 1962 as a work camps organization registered under the Societies’ Act. In 1993, KVDA was registered as a Non-Governmental organization by the establishment of the NGOs Coordination Act It is important to note that the outbound volunteering program is an arduous process with several stages, obstacles, processes and results; that requires versatile people who are dedicated to make a difference in the world. We have launched the applications for 2021 programs and we are short-listing potential volunteers for this program who want to volunteer abroad. Therefore, volunteers should be psychologically prepared aware that overseas travel is often stifled by the rigid visa regimes by various foreign missions. Kindly ensure that the documentation is sufficiently handled and KVDA will render every support for the successful implementation of the process. For those who have travelled overseas before, your track record will influence visa issuance. Kindly contact KVDA for application details for projects whose duration ranges from one month to one year.
